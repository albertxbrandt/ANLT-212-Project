# Rename to PantryPal

Date: 2026-10-07

The app name is PantryPal. The home count that was labeled "Lots on hand" is labeled "Items on hand".

An item is one stocking in a place, with its own quantity and expiry. A product is the catalog entry, such as Milk. Two purchases of milk are two items and one product, which is why the home screen keeps both numbers.
